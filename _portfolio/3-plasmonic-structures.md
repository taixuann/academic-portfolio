## Research Question & Purpose
How do core-shell composition ratios and inter-particle gap geometries modulate near-field hotspot enhancement and coupled photothermal dissipation in Au@Ag nanosphere dimer arrays?

## Project Summary
Localized surface plasmon resonance (LSPR) enables sub-diffraction optical field localization and photothermal conversion. This project investigates electrodynamic near-field enhancement and coupled nanoscale heat dissipation in Au and Au@Ag core-shell nanosphere arrays using 3D finite-difference time-domain (FDTD) and finite-element thermal modeling.

## My Contribution
* Constructed 3D full-wave electrodynamic models in Lumerical FDTD for Au and core-shell Au@Ag nanosphere dimer configurations.
* Calculated wavelength-dependent extinction, scattering, and absorption cross-sections across varying shell thicknesses (1–10 nm) and dielectric environments.
* Coupled optical absorption power profiles directly into Lumerical HEAT to simulate nanoscale steady-state and transient temperature distributions.
