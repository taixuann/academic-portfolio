## Key Research Highlights
* Demonstrated biopolymer-based crossbar memristive devices using nanoscale polydopamine (PDA) thin films.
* Unraveled the electric field-driven proton-coupled electron transfer (PCET) mechanism governing analog resistance states.
* Performed temperature-dependent electrical transport, fast-pulse characterization, and synaptic plasticity emulation (STP/PPF).
* Verified redox chemical states and interfacial proton dynamics using multi-technique spectroscopic characterization (XPS, Raman, UV–Vis).
