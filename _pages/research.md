My research operates at the intersection of **experimental device physics**, **functional biomaterials**, and **custom scientific instrumentation**.

---

### Thrust 1: Interfacial Proton-Coupled Electron Transfer in Biopolymer Memristors
Investigating how electric-field-induced proton migration and redox state transitions govern resistance switching in biopolymer thin films. Using polydopamine (PDA) crossbar junctions, we study analog weight updating, paired-pulse facilitation (PPF), and short-term synaptic plasticity for neuromorphic computing applications.

*See detailed case study: [Polydopamine-based Crossbar Devices](/portfolio/1-polydopamine-crossbar-devices/)*

---

### Thrust 2: Modular Cryogenic Instrumentation for Electronic Transport
Developing open-architecture, low-vibration characterization instruments capable of stable electrical measurements from 77 K (liquid nitrogen) to 350 K. The platform integrates finite-difference thermal modeling, custom cryogenic PCBs, and low-noise triaxial wiring for high-impedance device characterization.

*See detailed case study: [Low-Temperature Cryostat System](/portfolio/2-low-temperature-cryostat-system/)*

---

### Thrust 3: Computational Nanophotonics & Photothermal Modeling
Employing 3D Finite-Difference Time-Domain (Lumerical FDTD) electrodynamic simulations and finite-element thermal solvers (Lumerical HEAT) to design plasmonic transducers. We study localized surface plasmon resonance (LSPR) tuning in Au@Ag core-shell nanosphere dimers and quantify nanoscale photothermal heat dissipation.

*See detailed case study: [Plasmonic Structures](/portfolio/3-plasmonic-structures/)*
