I am a materials science and device physics researcher focused on **emerging electronic materials, resistive switching physics, and scientific instrumentation**.

My research investigates the interfacial physics of neuromorphic biopolymer memristors—specifically elucidating field-driven proton-coupled electron transfer (PCET) in polydopamine junctions—alongside developing modular cryogenic characterization systems (77 K–350 K) and electrodynamic modeling of plasmonic nanostructures.

---

## Research Focus

* **Bio-inspired & Biopolymer Memristors**: Interfacial proton-coupled electron transfer, volatile and non-volatile resistive switching mechanisms, and artificial synaptic emulation.
* **Scientific Instrumentation & Cryogenics**: Design of modular variable-temperature electrical transport platforms (77 K–350 K), low-noise PCB interfaces, and thermal finite-difference modeling.
* **Electrodynamic & Thermal Modeling**: 3D Lumerical FDTD and coupled Lumerical HEAT simulations of plasmonic nanostructure arrays.
