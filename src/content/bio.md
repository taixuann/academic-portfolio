I am a researcher in **Advanced Materials Science and Nanotechnology** and recent graduate from the University of Science and Technology of Hanoi (USTH), where I graduated as **class valedictorian** (Rank 1st, GPA 17.01/20.00).

My research focuses on **physical electronics**, **interfacial charge dynamics**, and **biomolecular memristive devices** for neuromorphic computing.

---

## Research Overview

At the **Laboratory of Advanced Materials and Application (LAMA, USTH)** under the supervision of Dr. Nguyen Van Quyen, I investigate how electrochemical protonation and interfacial chemical states govern volatile and analog switching in electrodeposited polydopamine (PDA) crossbar junctions.

During a visiting research appointment at the **Molecular Electronics Lab** at Guangdong Technion Israel Institute of Technology (GTIIT), I examined temporal synaptic dynamics and implemented custom pulse-endurance characterization routines, demonstrating stable interfacial proton-coupled electron transfer (PCET) switching across $10^6$ cycles.

In parallel with device physics, my work emphasizes custom experimental instrumentation and computational modeling:
- **Cryogenic Instrumentation:** Designed and validated a modular 80 K low-temperature cryostat system with Python-based transient thermal modeling.
- **Multiphysics & Optical Modeling:** Simulated optical extinction and photothermal dissipation in plasmonic nanosphere arrays using Lumerical FDTD and finite-difference heat solvers.
- **Automated Characterization:** Developed modular LabVIEW and Python routines for fast-pulse electrical testing, arbitrary waveforms, and synaptic plasticity emulation (STP/PPF).

I am currently preparing for doctoral studies starting in Fall 2026, aiming to connect fundamental interfacial physics with functional, bio-realistic neuromorphic hardware.

## Research Interests

- **Neuromorphic Devices & Synaptic Emulation:** Organic memristors, short-term plasticity (STP), paired-pulse facilitation (PPF), and bio-realistic physical electronics.
- **Interfacial Switching Physics:** Proton-coupled electron transfer (PCET), electrochemical redox kinetics, and conductive state relaxation dynamics.
- **Cryogenic & Electrical Instrumentation:** Variable-temperature transport, custom vacuum/cryostat design, and automated pulse measurement systems.
- **Multiphysics Modeling & Simulation:** Python finite-difference thermal analysis, FDTD optical modeling, and electrodynamic transport.
