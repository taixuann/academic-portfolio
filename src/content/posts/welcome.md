Welcome to my personal academic portfolio. This site showcases ongoing research in advanced materials science and nanotechnology, focusing on:

- **Polydopamine Memristive Devices:** Investigating interfacial proton-coupled electron transfer (PCET) in biopolymer thin films for neuromorphic computing.
- **Low-Temperature Instrumentation:** Modular cryostat development for cryogenic electrical transport characterization.
- **Computational Electrodynamics:** Numerical FDTD and photothermal modeling of plasmonic nanostructures.
