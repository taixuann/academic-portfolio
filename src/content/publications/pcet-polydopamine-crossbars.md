## Research Summary

Demonstrating electric field-driven interfacial proton-coupled electron transfer (PCET) in biopolymer crossbar junctions ($\text{Au/PDA/Pt}$) to achieve analog resistive switching and biological synaptic emulation.

## Key Findings

- Unraveled the electric field-driven proton-coupled electron transfer (PCET) mechanism governing analog resistance states in nanoscale polydopamine (PDA) active layers.
- Evaluated temperature-dependent electrical transport ($77\text{ K}\text{--}300\text{ K}$), compliance-dependent switching, and fast-pulse synaptic emulation including paired-pulse facilitation (PPF) and short-term plasticity (STP).
- Correlated catechol/quinone redox state transitions with memristive conduction states via XPS and Raman spectroscopy.
